HOW TO PLAY

New to Sudoku or SudoKai? Learn the rules and master the tools.

🎯

THE GOAL

Fill every cell in the 9×9 grid so that each row, column, and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 exactly once. No repeats allowed!

❤️

LIVES SYSTEM

You start with 3 lives (♥ ♥ ♥). Every wrong number costs you one life. Lose all 3 and the game ends. Use hints wisely to preserve your lives!

⚡

COMBO SYSTEM

Fill correct numbers consecutively to build combos! Combos multiply your XP reward and trigger special animations. Keep the streak going!

🏆

XP & LEVELS

Earn XP for every correct cell. Harder difficulties give more XP. Solve full puzzles for a bonus. Level up to unlock bragging rights on the leaderboard!

HOW TO SOLVE — STEP BY STEP

1

Start with what you know

Look for rows, columns, or boxes that already have many numbers filled in. The more clues, the fewer possibilities remain for empty cells.

2

Use the Notes tool

Tap NOTES to enter pencil marks — small numbers showing what's possible in a cell. This helps you track candidates without committing to a wrong answer.

3

Look for naked singles

If a cell has only one possible number after eliminating conflicts from its row, column, and box — that's the answer! Fill it in with confidence.

4

Scan for hidden singles

If a digit can only fit in one cell within a row, column, or box — even if that cell has other candidates — place it there. This is the most powerful basic technique.

5

When stuck, ask KAI

Click AI HINT to get an intelligent hint from Claude AI. KAI will analyze the board and suggest the best next move with a clear explanation.
